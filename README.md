# Future Black Female (FBF) — Human-Centric Multi-Template Platform

A production-quality React application featuring an **Executive Review Portal** and **Three Distinct Human-Centric Website Concepts** built with **React (JSX only)**, **Tailwind CSS**, and authentic brand assets crawled directly from [futureblackfemale.com](https://www.futureblackfemale.com/).

---

## 🏛️ Design Transformation & Human-Centric Philosophy

Following executive review and feedback to eliminate artificial AI-generated clichés (excessive gradients, glassmorphism, floating blur circles, overused rounded-3xl bubbles, and SaaS dashboard matrixes), the entire platform was redesigned to be:

* **Human-first, not AI-first**: Natural layouts, authentic typography hierarchy, and purposeful whitespace.
* **Business-oriented**: Built around the actual operations of Future Black Female (registered Canadian charity # 79425 9531 RR0001).
* **Authentic to the live identity**: The live website (`futureblackfemale.com`) is the primary source of truth for visual identity, photography, and copy.
* **Exact Content Preserved**: No fabricated statistics or services. All 12 real programs, board members, research whitepapers, media coverage, and legal disclosures are integrated end-to-end.

### 1. Concept 1: The Authentic Brand Original
* **Core Idea**: Direct elevation and modernization of the live `futureblackfemale.com` website.
* **Palette**: Warm Cream (`#FAF8F5`), Brand Gold/Ochre (`#D48B28`), Deep Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`), Terracotta (`#8C3A27`), and Pure White.
* **Typography**: Classic editorial serif (`Playfair Display` / `Georgia`) paired with highly legible sans-serif body text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
* **Signature Components**:
  * Top Announcement Bar: *Future Black Trailblazers Award: $500 Scholarship for Black Students going to Brock U this fall.* -> `[Wow Us!]`
  * Authentic Top Utility Header: `Log In`, `DONATE`, `EN | FR` language switcher, `Melanin & Manuscripts Book Club`, and `Cart (0)`.
  * Official FBF Logo (`header_logo.png`).
  * Hero Section: High-resolution photo (`hero_darina.jpg`), exact headline *"Privileging Black girls and women across Canada and globally."*, `[Learn More]`, and `[donate now]`.
  * Authentic Intro: *"FUTURE BLACK FEMALE — Where Black women increase access to opportunities for Black female youth."* with real photo of two Black women smiling (`about_two_women.jpg`).
  * The 3 Pillars: Pro-Black Mental Health Services (`pillar_mental_health.jpg`), Financial Wellbeing (`pillar_financial.jpg`), Career & Education Strategies (`pillar_career.jpg`) with `LEARN MORE` links.
  * Engagement Motivation: *"The well-being of Black female youth is our motivation."* with authentic step icons (`step_1.png`, `step_2.png`, `step_3.png`).
  * Funders Section: Verified Canadian funding partners (Canadian Women's Foundation, Niagara Community Foundation, Women and Gender Equality Canada) with `[GET IN TOUCH]`.
  * From Our Interns: Verified testimonials with authentic intern photos (`intern_nappy.jpg`, `intern_bestbe.jpg`).
  * Number Ring Pagination System for events (first 5 then 5).
  * Full Land Acknowledgement, Charity # 79425 9531 RR0001, and Stella the Studio branding credit.

### 2. Concept 2: The Modern Civic Institution
* **Core Idea**: Authoritative philanthropic foundation & public policy layout (Ford Foundation, MacArthur Foundation style).
* **Palette**: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), Cool Slate Gray (`#F8FAFC`), Deep Navy/Slate (`#0F172A`), and restrained Ochre Gold (`#C88728`).
* **Typography**: Disciplined grotesque sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) with clear, legible hierarchy.
* **Signature Components**:
  * Clean institutional top bar with direct Annual Report download link and CRA charity number.
  * Executive mission briefing with verified 3-pillar metric strip (1,750+ sessions, 400+ youth, 200+ mentors).
  * Structured wide-format Strategic Pillars with programmatic scope and service boundaries.
  * Formal Institutional Supporter table with Canadian grant partners.
  * Graduate research fellow testimonials with placement roles and institutional citations.
  * Structured document repository table for audited filings, AGM proceedings, and strategic roadmaps.
  * Civic schedule view for upcoming and past events with Number Ring Pagination.

### 3. Concept 3: Warm Editorial & Feminine Modern
* **Core Idea**: Warm, welcoming, community-first editorial publication (inspired by premium non-profit publications like *Voices United* and *Blossoms of Hope*).
* **Palette**: Warm Ivory (`#FFFDF9`), Soft Blush (`#F7E8E8`), Muted Burgundy (`#873F50`), Dusty Terracotta (`#D68D82`), and Deep Espresso (`#2A1D21`).
* **Typography**: Literary serif titles (`Playfair Display`) with spacious humanist body text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
* **Signature Components**:
  * Clean `FBF.` monogram with burgundy dot, `ABOUT PROGRAMS IMPACT EVENTS` links, and Burgundy `DONATE ↗` button.
  * Top micro-announcement bar with charity number and Melanin & Manuscripts book club shortcut.
  * Hero Section:
    * Eyebrow: `EMPOWERING THE NEXT GENERATION`
    * Headline: *"A future where every Black girl can thrive."*
    * Subheadline: *"Creating access to opportunities, community, and economic empowerment for Black girls and young women aged 16–22."*
    * CTAs: `Explore our work ↗` and `Our mission`
    * Featured Photo: Young Black women in city park (`hero-feminine.jpg`).
    * Three-Card Bento Metric Strip: `1,750+` sessions, `400+` youth, `200+` mentors.
  * WHAT WE DO: Opportunities that create lasting change bento grid for Mental Wellbeing, Financial Wellbeing, and Career Development.
  * Authentic Canadian funder logos (CWF, NCF, WAGE) & intern testimonials.
  * Events with Number Ring Pagination, research hub, and donation impact tiers.

## 🔄 Design Review Toolbar

A discreet, human-designed review toolbar is pinned at the bottom of the screen. Reviewers can:
* Switch instantaneously between **Overview Hub**, **1. Authentic Brand**, **2. Civic Institution**, and **3. Warm Editorial** without page reload.
* Test the interactive donation modal.
* **Collapse / Minimize** the toolbar anytime to inspect full headers, banners, or footers with zero visual obstruction.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **Framework**: React 19 (**JSX only** — strictly no TypeScript or TSX)
* **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 (`@tailwindcss/vite`)
* **Code Splitting**: Dynamic imports via `React.lazy()` and `<Suspense>` for optimal sub-second load times.
* **Icons**: `lucide-react`
* **Assets**: High-resolution authentic photos and logos crawled from `futureblackfemale.com`.

In your project directory (`p:\git\female`):

2. **Production Preview Build**:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   npm run preview
   ```
   Open `http://localhost:4173/` in your browser.
